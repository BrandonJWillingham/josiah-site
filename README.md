# Josiah — Modeling & Music Portfolio

A personal portfolio and digital business card showcasing my professional modeling work and experience as a live and session bass player.

## About This Project

This website serves as my personal **digital business card**, bringing together two sides of my professional work: **fashion modeling and music**.

The site provides a central place for clients, agencies, photographers, musicians, and other collaborators to view selected modeling campaigns, watch live bass performances, learn more about my musical background, and get in contact for bookings.

Rather than creating separate websites for each part of my work, I designed the platform to present both disciplines through a single responsive and visually focused experience.

The homepage highlights selected modeling work alongside featured musical performances, while dedicated sections provide additional information for modeling and live/session bass inquiries.

## Modeling

The modeling portfolio showcases selected commercial, editorial, and e-commerce work from brands and campaigns I have worked with.

Featured work includes projects for brands such as:

- ONS
- Moon Juice
- Hollister
- U.S. Polo Assn.
- David Beckham
- Reason Clothing

The site also provides direct access to professional booking information for modeling inquiries.

## Music

The music section showcases my work as a **live and session bass player**.

I began playing music at a young age, starting with piano before moving to bass at eight years old. My playing developed through gospel music and later expanded into R&B and soul.

The portfolio includes:

- Live performance videos
- Performance reels
- Musical background and biography
- Bass equipment information
- Live and session booking inquiries

My playing style focuses on a strong pocket, warm tone, melodic movement, and serving the song.

## Key Features

- Responsive modeling portfolio

- Professional modeling booking links

- Mobile-friendly video reels
